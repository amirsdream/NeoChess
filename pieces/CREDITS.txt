Staunton piece drawings by Colin M. L. Burnett.

The SVG outlines in this folder are the Cburnett chess set, used here as
recolorable silhouettes. They are triple-licensed under the GNU Free
Documentation License, the BSD license, and the GNU General Public License.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces
A matching copy is published with Lichess under GPLv2 or later:
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett

NeoChess recolors the outlines at runtime. It does not include the original
colored PNG renders.
